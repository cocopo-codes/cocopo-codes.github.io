## Why it works

<p class="pullquote">Most content writers can't verify what they publish. Most engineers don't have time to write. I've done both.</p>

After seven years of QA test automation and data engineering, then three years and 100+ technical articles for developer audiences, I know where AI-generated technical content quietly goes wrong. I use AI tools to move faster, and I bring the testing discipline that catches what they get wrong. Based in Madrid, working in European hours.
