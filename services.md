## FAQs

<details class="faq">
<summary>What types of companies do you work with?</summary>
<p>I work primarily with B2B SaaS companies, developer tool platforms, API providers, and tech startups that need to communicate complex technical concepts to developer audiences.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Do you use AI tools in your work?</summary>
<p>Yes, deliberately and transparently. I use AI for research, outlining, and first-draft acceleration, and then I verify everything and add my own human style and touch. My seven years in QA test automation and data engineering mean I test the code, validate the API calls, and confirm technical claims before anything ships. AI makes me faster; the QA background is what keeps it accurate.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How do you ensure technical accuracy?</summary>
<p>I have seven years of data engineering, QA test automation, and some web development under my belt, plus three years writing technical documentation and content. I work closely with teams and subject matter experts to verify technical details. I test code examples, validate API calls, and ensure all technical information is current and correct.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What's your process for creating technical content?</summary>
<p>My process starts with understanding your product, audience, and goals. I then conduct research, interview subject matter experts if needed, create a detailed brief with an outline that you will approve, and finally draft content that balances technical accuracy with readability. Every piece goes through multiple review cycles to ensure quality.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What's your turnaround time?</summary>
<p>Timelines vary by project scope. A short blog post on a topic I'm already an expert in takes 5–7 business days, while comprehensive documentation projects could be a 6-month or even 9-month project. I always negotiate timelines upfront according to the project's needs, and the business's general speed of communication. I will always communicate proactively about progress.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Do you offer content strategy services?</summary>
<p>Yes! Beyond writing, I help companies develop documentation strategies, conduct content audits, create style guides, and build scalable documentation systems.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
